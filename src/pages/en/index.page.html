About Me

I attended primary and intermediate school in Japan, and spent high school and my undergraduate years in New Zealand. I am currently researching optimisation problems related to cafeteria menu planning at Kobe University. During my undergraduate degree, I researched nonlinear constraints in a pseudo-boolean optimisation solver. My interests include SAT, NP-hard combinatorial optimisation problems, and the design and analysis of solvers solving these problems.

I enjoy competitive programming, and my AtCoder rating is in the cyan range. I also participated in the ICPC in 2023 and 2024.

Outside research and programming, I enjoy rhythm games. I play maimai and CHUNITHM, my maimai rating is around 15300, and my CHUNITHM rating is around 14.50.

Japanese and Cantonese are my native languages, and I use English at CEFR C1 level and Mandarin at B2 level.
